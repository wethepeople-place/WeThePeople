# Draft — U.S. Census Bureau item-level media clearance request

**Status:** For user review only. Not sent or submitted.

**Subject:** Request for reuse and accessibility details for four Census Bureau housing videos

Hello U.S. Census Bureau team,

WeThePeople.place is developing a nonpartisan, evidence-linked civic video experience. We are considering four Census Bureau housing videos for a development catalog, but we will not download, redistribute, or publish them unless the Bureau confirms the applicable rights and supplies or authorizes the necessary assets.

The four videos are:

1. [Tracking Housing Trends with the Housing Unit Change Viewer](https://www.census.gov/library/video/2025/tracking-housing-trends-with-housing-unit-change-viewer.html) — May 30, 2025.
2. [Explore Housing Data Insights: How to Find Key Housing Trends & Demographics](https://www.census.gov/data/academy/data-gems/2024/key-housing-trends-and-demographics.html) — December 12, 2024.
3. [Discover Your Neighborhood with the Current Census Address Count Listing Files Viewer](https://www.census.gov/data/academy/data-gems/2025/census-address-map-viewer.html) — June 26, 2025.
4. [How to Visualize Population and Housing Trends Between 2020 and 2010](https://www.census.gov/data/academy/data-gems/2021/how-to-visualize-population-and-housing-trends-between-2020-and-2010.html) — September 13, 2021.

For each item, could you please confirm or provide:

- whether the complete work was created by federal employees as part of their official duties, or the explicit license and reuse terms that apply;
- whether any video, narration, music, images, graphics, footage, trademarks, or other components are owned by or restricted by a third party, and any limits that apply to them;
- an authorized media file, download location, embed method, or other approved delivery method for reuse outside the Census Bureau page;
- a descriptive poster image and written authority to reuse it;
- the verified duration, pixel dimensions, and aspect ratio;
- an accessible caption track, its format and language, and whether it has received a caption-conformance review;
- confirmation that the published transcript has been reviewed for accuracy and accessibility;
- permitted uses, modification limits, required attribution, source-link wording, and any restrictions involving Census Bureau names or marks; and
- the name or role of the rights reviewer and accessibility reviewer, together with the date of each review or confirmation.

We would preserve links to the official Census Bureau page and supporting evidence, clearly identify the Bureau as the source, avoid implying endorsement, and follow any supplied attribution or use restrictions. If redistribution is not authorized, please let us know whether an official embed is permitted and what accessibility and poster assets may accompany it.

Written confirmation by reply, or referral to the office that can provide item-level clearance, would be appreciated. We can also provide our planned presentation and metadata fields if that would help your review.

## Internal send checklist

Before any outreach, the user must:

- approve the wording and choose the sender name and contact information;
- identify the correct official Census Bureau recipient or submission channel;
- explicitly authorize sending or submitting the request; and
- retain the sent request and complete response as provenance evidence.

No reply alone makes a candidate catalog-eligible unless it resolves every required item above or clearly identifies which items remain restricted or unknown.
