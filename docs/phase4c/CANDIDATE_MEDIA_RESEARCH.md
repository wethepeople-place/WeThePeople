# Phase 4C candidate civic-media research

## Result

Four U.S. Census Bureau housing videos are strong content candidates, but none is acceptance-ready or rights-cleared. No video, poster, caption file, or transcript was downloaded or copied. The shortlist records links only.

Federal law generally excludes works of the United States Government from domestic copyright protection, and Census policy says works created by Census employees generally are not protected in the United States. That does not establish item-level clearance. USAGov warns that federal pages may contain copyrighted material used with permission, and the Census policy expressly recognizes contractor-created or assigned works. The candidate pages use an embedded third-party player and do not expose an authorized reusable video asset, reusable poster, duration, dimensions, item-level authorship statement, or explicit license.

## Conditional shortlist

1. **Tracking Housing Trends with the Housing Unit Change Viewer** — May 30, 2025. Official Census landing page and official PDF transcript are present. It directly supports the Housing & Rent issue through housing-unit change and local planning evidence.
2. **Explore Housing Data Insights: How to Find Key Housing Trends & Demographics** — December 12, 2024. Official page and transcript are present, with links to the American Housing Survey and American Community Survey. It covers value, occupancy, tenure, expenses, and renter demographics.
3. **Discover Your Neighborhood with the Current Census Address Count Listing Files Viewer** — June 26, 2025. Official page and transcript are present. It covers local housing-unit counts and neighborhood change.
4. **How to Visualize Population and Housing Trends Between 2020 and 2010** — September 13, 2021. Official page and transcript are present. It connects decennial Census evidence to local housing change.

Each remains `catalog_eligible: false`. An official transcript supports accessibility review but does not prove that captions exist or conform. An embedded player supports viewing on the agency page but does not authorize downloading, redistributing, hotlinking, or using the platform thumbnail as a poster.

## Clearance needed

For each candidate, obtain written item-level confirmation of federal-employee authorship or an explicit license; confirmation that video, audio, music, imagery, and marks contain no restricted third-party components; an authorized media asset or delivery method; poster reuse authority; duration, dimensions, and aspect ratio; caption-track verification; allowed-use and attribution language; and named rights and accessibility reviews with timestamps.

Under the superseding hybrid source policy, these candidates may remain discoverable only as canonical link-out records until a source-level embed policy, license, or publisher agreement is approved in the source registry. Item-level outreach is not the default scaling path. No Census source or media is activated by this research record.
